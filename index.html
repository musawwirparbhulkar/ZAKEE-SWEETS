Our Story

More Than Just Sweets

“At ZAKEE, every sweet is made to turn an ordinary moment into something worth remembering.”

Every box that leaves our counter carries the same idea: mithai made properly, with patience, and without shortcuts. From rich, festive classics to lighter everyday favourites, each sweet is prepared with care and finished by hand.

Because at ZAKEE, sweets are never just something you eat — they’re something you share. Whether you’re picking up a small treat for today or a box for a celebration that matters, that idea shapes everything we make.

Our Team

The Hands Behind the Sweets

Every piece of mithai at ZAKEE is made by people who care about getting it right. Our halwais have spent years perfecting their craft — from the first boil of the day to the last box packed for a wedding.

When you visit, you’ll see the same faces — and taste the same consistency — that built our reputation. That’s not a tagline; it’s how we work.
